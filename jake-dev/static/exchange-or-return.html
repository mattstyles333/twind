Exchanges & Returns

100% Satisfaction Guarantee

We want you to absolutely love your new glasses! That's why we offer:

  • 30-day money-back guarantee if you're not happy for any reason.
  • 12-month frame warranty.

Whether it's the frame colour, fit, or your prescription doesn't feel right, we'll happily replace or refund your order in full, no questions asked.

How Our Guarantee Works

To make sure our prices stay fair for everyone, our guarantee covers genuine changes of mind or customer errors:

  • Your first return or exchange is free within 30 days of receiving your glasses.
  • To prevent misuse, each customer is eligible for one free return or exchange every 6 months.

If you need additional exchanges or corrections after your free exchange:

  • 20% of the frame cost applies.
  • 50% of any replacement lens cost applies.
  • If you choose to return an order for a refund after using your free exchange, the above fees will be deducted from your refund.
  • Return postage and postage for sending replacement glasses back to you are not included.

Note: If you choose store credit instead of a refund, we will reimburse your return postage costs, up to £5 for UK orders or up to £15 for international orders. Proof of postage is required.

If We've Made a Mistake

If there's a manufacturing fault, prescription error, or quality issue caused by us, we will remake or replace your glasses at no cost, including postage.

Christmas Gift Returns Policy

If you are purchasing your glasses as a gift for Christmas, we are pleased to offer an extended returns window until January 25th.

Please Note: To receive this extended return period, you must contact us to formally request this extension so we can add it to your order. The extension is not applied automatically.

How to Return Your Glasses

Contact Us First – Before returning your glasses, please email us at info@spex4less.com or call us on 0151 632 6611 during working hours (Monday to Friday, 09:00–17:00, excluding Bank Holidays).

Include a Cover Letter – Once your return is confirmed, please enclose a cover letter with your order details, including your name, order number, and reason for return. This ensures we can process your request quickly.

Important Return Information

When returning an item, we recommend obtaining proof of postage from the Post Office, as we cannot take responsibility for lost packages.

If the return is due to an error by Spex4Less, we will reimburse postage costs upon request, limited to the amount paid on your original order.

All glasses returned for reasons other than faulty frames or lenses must be in original condition, including accessories, cases, cloths, branded boxes, and any other brand materials included with your order.
