Common Questions

Simply contact us within 30 days of receiving your glasses. We'll arrange a full refund once we receive them back - no questions asked. This applies whether you don't like the style, they don't fit right, or you've changed your mind.

Our warranty covers all manufacturing defects including frame defects, coating issues, lens delamination, loose screws, and any prescription errors from our side. We'll repair or replace them free of charge.

For just £20, it covers accidents the warranty doesn't - scratched lenses, broken frames from drops, or even sitting on your glasses. If you're active, wear your glasses daily, or are prone to accidents, this plan can save you hundreds.

Contact us by phone, email, or live chat. For warranty claims, we'll assess and arrange repair or replacement. For accidental damage, just describe what happened and send photos. We process most claims within 24-48 hours.

For manufacturing errors or warranty claims, we provide a free returns label. For satisfaction guarantee returns, customers cover return shipping. We recommend a tracked service for peace of mind.
