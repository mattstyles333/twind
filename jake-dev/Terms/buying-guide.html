How to measure: Check the inside arm of your current glasses for 3 numbers (e.g. 54-18-140). For guidance use our Size Guide.

Good to Know

Frame width is the most reliable measurement, though lens diameter works just as well. Staying within ±2mm of your current lenses ensures a similar fit.

Choosing Your Lenses

With frames selected, the next step is lenses. Our checkout lets you build a custom package so you never pay for extras you don't need. Here is how to choose the right ones.

Which Lenses Do I Need?

Explore our complete range of prescription lenses. Whether you need standard single vision, advanced varifocals, or specialist occupational lenses, use the guide below to match the right lens type to your vision needs.

Lens Types

Single Vision

The standard choice for most, correcting one field of vision. Whether you need reading glasses, driving glasses, or computer glasses, you can customise the lens to match your day-to-day.

Explore Single Vision Glasses

Bifocal

A classic dual-focus lens with a visible line. Perfect for users who need clear distance and reading vision without the peripheral distortion sometimes found in varifocals.

Shop bifocal glasses

Varifocal (Progressive)

A seamless blend of distance, intermediate, and reading power with no visible line. The best choice for everyday convenience if you want to see clearly at all distances without constantly swapping glasses.

Shop varifocal glasses

Occupational

Optimised specifically for desk and screen work. These lenses offer a much wider intermediate zone than standard varifocals, perfect for viewing computers and documents comfortably.

Shop occupational glasses
