How Our Guarantee Works

To make sure our prices stay fair for everyone, our guarantee covers genuine changes of mind or customer errors for prescription orders:

Your First Exchange is Free

  • ✓ Your first return or exchange is free within 30 days of receiving your glasses.
  • To prevent misuse, each customer is eligible for one free return or exchange every 6 months.

Additional Exchanges

If you need additional exchanges or corrections after your free exchange:

  • 20% of the frame cost applies.
  • 50% of any replacement lens cost applies.
  • If you choose to return an order for a refund after using your free exchange, the above fees will be deducted from your refund.
  • Return postage and postage for sending replacement glasses back to you are not included.

Note: If you choose store credit instead of a refund, we will reimburse your return postage costs, up to the same value as the original postage paid on purchase.

If We've Made a Mistake

We'll Fix It Free of Charge

If there's a manufacturing fault, prescription error, or quality issue caused by us, we will remake or replace your glasses at no cost, including postage.

Non-Custom Orders

For "Frame Only" orders or non-prescription sunglasses (items supplied with dummy lenses or manufacturer lenses):

  • Statutory Period: You have a statutory right to cancel your order for a full refund within 14 days of receipt.
  • Condition: The goods must be returned in exactly the condition they were received.
  • Packaging: You must return the item in its original, undamaged branded packaging (box, case, cloth). If the packaging is torn, written on, or damaged, we reserve the right to deduct an amount from your refund to cover the diminished value of the goods (up to 100% of the value if the item is not resaleable).
