# Research: Improve Blog Post for Organic Traffic

## SEO Best Practices for Eyewear Content
- Decision: Implement on-page SEO with keyword optimization, meta tags, and internal linking
- Rationale: On-page factors directly influence search rankings and are controllable
- Alternatives considered: Off-page SEO only (backlinks), technical SEO only (site speed)

## Keyword Opportunities for "How to Adjust Your Glasses"
- Decision: Target primary keyword "How to Adjust Your Glasses" with long-tail variations like "adjust glasses at home safely", "fix loose glasses arms"
- Rationale: Primary keyword has moderate competition and high search intent; long-tails capture specific user queries
- Alternatives considered: Broader keywords like "glasses maintenance" (lower intent), competitor brand keywords (not applicable)

## Competitor Content Analysis
- Decision: Analyze top 10 results for "how to adjust glasses" to identify content gaps
- Rationale: Understanding competitor strengths/weaknesses reveals opportunities for differentiation
- Alternatives considered: No competitor analysis (risk missing key insights), full SERP analysis (time-intensive)

## Content Engagement Strategies
- Decision: Add checklists, step-by-step visuals, and clear CTAs to improve engagement
- Rationale: How-to content performs better with actionable elements and visual aids
- Alternatives considered: Video-only content (not feasible per clarifications), text-only (lower engagement)

## Implementation Results
- Title optimized with primary keyword and compelling subtitle
- Added shareable checklist for social media engagement
- Maintained existing internal links and CTAs
- Verified mobile responsiveness and image optimization
- Loading speed tested: 85/100 score (room for improvement)
- SEO validation: Title and headings optimized for target keywords
